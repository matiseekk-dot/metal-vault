## ASO (App Store Optimization) keywords

Google ranks Play Store apps using:
1. App name (highest weight)
2. Short description
3. Long description
4. Reviews mentioning keywords

**Primary keywords to target** (high search volume, manageable competition):
- "vinyl tracker" (medium volume, low competition)
- "vinyl collection app" (medium/medium)
- "record collection tracker" (medium/medium)
- "vinyl insurance" (low/very low — easy win)
- "vinyl price tracker" (low/low)

**Secondary keywords** (work into long description naturally):
- "discogs alternative"
- "music collection inventory"
- "homeowner insurance music"
- "record value tracker"
- "limited edition vinyl alerts"

**Avoid**:
- Single-word keywords like "metal" (impossible to rank, too broad)
- "best vinyl app" — Apple/Google penalize comparative claims

## Initial review strategy

**First 50 reviews matter most** for ranking.

Ask happy users to leave reviews:
- After they've used the app for **14 days minimum** (not at sign-up)
- After a "win moment" (e.g. completed insurance PDF, found a repress alert)
- Send via in-app prompt: "Loving Metal Vault? A Play Store review helps other collectors find us 🤘"

**Don't**:
- Ask for review on day 1
- Use fake reviews (Google detects, blacklists app)
- Offer rewards for reviews (against Play Store policy)
