# Instagram Content Calendar — Metal Vault Launch

## Strategy

Instagram is the **lowest-conversion** of the three channels (Reddit/FB/IG) for utility apps, but it's the **best brand-building** channel. Goal: build a recognizable @metalvault account that creates organic awareness and trust over 30+ days.

**Key insight**: Instagram users won't sign up after one post. They follow your account, see consistent content, and convert weeks/months later when they have a need. Plant seeds now, harvest in 3-6 months.

**Account setup**:
- Handle: `@metalvault.app` (or `@metalvaultapp` if taken)
- Bio: `Vinyl tracker for metal collectors 🤘 // Insurance reports • Smart alerts • Photo docs // [link to app]`
- Profile pic: app logo (red circle, "MV" mark)
- Highlights: "Features", "How-to", "Behind the build", "User collections"

**Post types** (rotation):
1. **Vinyl photos** with feature callout (60% of posts)
2. **App screenshots** with use case (20%)
3. **Behind-the-scenes / dev journey** (10%)
4. **User-generated content** when you have it (10%)

## Reels strategy

Reels get **5-10x more reach** than static posts in 2026. Aim for **2 Reels per week**.

**Reel topics that work**:
1. **Quick screen recordings** of app features (15-30 sec, music = lo-fi or instrumental metal)
2. **"Day in the life of a metal collector"** — vinyl haul, listening session, app showcase woven in
3. **Quick tutorials** — "How to scan a barcode at a record store" (very specific, very useful)
4. **Reaction content** — "Reacting to my own collection's value over 6 months"

**Format**:
- Hook in first 1.5 seconds
- Show value in first 5 seconds
- Loop seamlessly (last frame matches first frame)
- On-screen captions (60% of users watch muted)

---

## Engagement reciprocity

Best ROI tactic on Instagram for niche accounts: **comment on 20 relevant accounts per day** with genuine interest.

Targets:
- Big metal accounts (BangerTV, Loudwire, Metal Hammer)
- Mid-tier metal labels (Profound Lore, Season of Mist)
- Vinyl reviewer accounts (search "#vinylreview")
- Other metalheads' collections (#metalvinyl tag)

**Comment format**: 1-2 sentences, specific reference to their post, ZERO mention of your app. The goal is to be a recognizable face in the community.

After 30 days of this, your engagement rate will 3-5x organically.

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Buy followers (algorithm penalizes, conversion is zero)
- ❌ Use more than 30 hashtags (shadowban)
- ❌ Post stock photos (community spots fakes immediately)
- ❌ Use AI-generated images for your "collection" (people can tell)
- ❌ Constantly DM strangers your link (Instagram bans this fast)
- ❌ Cross-post Reddit content verbatim (different platforms = different tone)
- ❌ Post during low-engagement times (3-5am local time, 11pm-7am)

---

## Goals & metrics for month 1

Realistic targets:
- **Followers**: 200-500
- **Average post engagement**: 5-15%
- **Reels views**: 300-2000 per Reel (after first 5 to learn algorithm)
- **App signups attributable**: 20-50 (very low compared to Reddit, but quality)
- **DMs/inquiries**: 5-15

If you hit these, **Instagram is working as planned**. Don't expect viral hits in month 1.

---

## Long-term play

Instagram is the channel that pays off in **months 6-18**, not month 1. Consistency matters more than virality. **One mediocre post per week for 12 months > 5 amazing posts in week 1 then nothing.**

If you can't sustain 3-4 posts per week + daily community engagement, scale back to **2 posts per week max**. Better to underdeliver consistently than overdeliver and burn out.
