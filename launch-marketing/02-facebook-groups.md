# Facebook Groups — Metal Vault Launch

## Strategy

Facebook groups skew older than Reddit (35-50 demographic). Many active metal/vinyl groups have **5-50k members** and high engagement. Conversion is slower than Reddit but **retention is better** — older collectors stick around longer.

**Key rule**: every group has its own moderator culture. Read pinned posts before posting. Some groups ban any external links; others welcome them. **Always ask the mod first** if rules are ambiguous — costs nothing, builds goodwill.

**Posting cadence**: 1-2 groups per day for 5-7 days. Don't blast all 10 in 24h.

---

## Tier 1: Best fit (post FIRST)

These groups are actively engaged metal collectors who would directly benefit. Highest conversion expected.

### 1. **r/Metal Vinyl Trading** (Facebook group, ~25k members)
- **Vibe**: Active trading + showcase. Collectors who care about rarity and value.
- **Best post angle**: "Built insurance documentation tool — useful for traders too"
- **Notes**: Mods are strict on commercial posts. Frame as "feedback wanted from active traders".

### 2. **Vinyl Records — Heavy Metal Edition** (~18k)
- **Vibe**: International (heavy DACH presence). English posts welcome.
- **Best angle**: "Cross-platform price tracking for our collections"
- **Notes**: Active comment threads on Mondays-Wednesdays.

### 3. **Black Metal Vinyl Collectors** (~12k)
- **Vibe**: Niche-within-niche. Obsessive about pressing details.
- **Best angle**: "Smart alerts for repress detection and limited variants"
- **Notes**: They will roast generic apps. Lead with rarity-tracking features.

### 4. **Death Metal Vinyl Enthusiasts** (~10k)
- **Vibe**: Old-school OG hunters. High engagement on rare finds.
- **Best angle**: "OG vs reissue tracking, condition photo documentation"
- **Notes**: Post a screenshot of *your own* OG collection alongside the app pitch.

### 5. **Doom & Stoner Vinyl** (~8k)
- **Vibe**: Slower, thoughtful, willing to read long posts.
- **Best angle**: "Long-term value tracking — I built a tool for collectors who hold for years"
- **Notes**: Less skeptical of indie devs than mainstream metal groups.

## Tier 2: Good fit (post SECOND, days 4-7)

### 6. **Vinyl Collectors Worldwide** (~80k, mixed genres)
- **Vibe**: Big tent, lots of newbies + veterans mixed
- **Best angle**: Generic "vinyl tracking app, free + Pro tier" — keep it simple
- **Conversion**: Lower (audience is 30% metal at most), but volume helps

### 7. **Heavy Metal Vinyl** (~35k, US-skewing)
- **Vibe**: US collectors, includes 80s glam crowd
- **Best angle**: Insurance reports (homeowner's insurance is bigger US concern)
- **Notes**: Avoid black metal references — too niche for this audience

### 8. **Heavy Metal Plattensammler** (~6k, German-language)
- **Vibe**: German DACH collectors, very serious about preservation
- **Best angle**: Photo documentation + insurance docs (German insurance is detailed)
- **Notes**: Post in English with German context: "App is in English but pricing converts to EUR automatically"
- **Caution**: Some Germans dislike English-only apps. Note that more languages are planned.

### 9. **Metal Vinyl UK** (~7k)
- **Vibe**: UK-specific, Bandcamp/Discogs sellers active
- **Best angle**: Multi-currency support, eBay UK price tracking
- **Notes**: Mention Bristol/Birmingham scene specifically if relevant

### 10. **Polish Metal Vinyl Collectors / Metalowy Winyl Polska** (~3k, PL)
- **Vibe**: Polish speakers, your home base
- **Best angle**: "Built by a Polish dev, English UI but supports collection from anywhere"
- **Notes**: This is your ONE Polish-language post. Even though app is English-only now, the personal connection works.

## Universal post template

Customize for each group, but the structure stays:

```
[Personal greeting referencing the group's specific vibe]
"Hey [group name] — long-time member, first commercial-ish post."

[Credibility paragraph — show you're a real collector, not a marketer]
"My collection is around [X] records, mostly [genres]. Recent grail: [specific record]. I've been using [Discogs/Excel/CLZ] for tracking."

[The frustration]
"Last [winter/quarter/etc.] I needed to update my insurance documentation. Took me weeks. Couldn't find a tool that did [specific thing]."

[The solution]
"So I built one. Metal Vault. Web app, Android via Play Store soon. Here's what it does that I couldn't find elsewhere:
- [3-4 features as bullet points]
- Free tier covers [specific use case]"

[Honest disclaimer]
"It's solo-built, so there are bugs. Open to feedback. Not trying to replace Discogs."

[The ask]
"If anyone wants to test it, link below. Just looking for honest reactions before pushing to public launch."

[Link]

[Soft close]
"Mods, taking down if not allowed."
```

**Length**: 150-300 words is the sweet spot. Longer = scroll past. Shorter = looks like spam.

## Outreach (not posting) — direct messages to group admins

For Tier 1 groups, **before posting**, send a friendly DM to the admin/mod:

```
Hi [name],

Long-time member of [group]. I've built a vinyl tracking app focused on metal collectors and would love to share it for feedback. Before posting, I want to make sure it's OK with the group rules.

Quick context: [1-line about app] + [1-line about your collector cred].

Would a feedback-focused post be welcome? Happy to share the draft for your review first. Not selling — just looking for community input before public launch.

Thanks for moderating this group, it's been a great community.

[Your name]
```

**Hit rate**: ~50% reply, ~80% of those are welcoming. The 20% who say no are doing you a favor — saves you a ban.

## Engagement after posting

For each group post:
- **Day 0-1**: Reply to every comment within 4 hours
- **Day 2-7**: Check daily, reply within 24h
- **Day 7+**: Stop pushing the post; move on

If post gets 10+ comments and 50+ likes, you can pin it to your own profile or share to other groups (carefully — don't spam).

If post gets <5 engagements, post once more in 30 days with different angle. Don't double-post within 30 days.

---

## What to avoid in FB groups

- **Don't post landing page links** if group rules ban them — use plain text + "DM for link"
- **Don't tag random members** — looks desperate
- **Don't argue with Discogs loyalists** — agree, position as complement
- **Don't run paid ads in groups** — moderators ban this on sight
- **Don't post after-hours** — algorithms favor engagement bursts; post when audience is active (8-10am or 6-9pm in target timezone)

## Tracking

Spreadsheet columns:
- Group name + member count
- Post date
- Likes after 24h / 7 days
- Comments after 24h / 7 days
- DM inquiries received
- Sign-ups attributable (UTM: `?utm_source=fb&utm_campaign=launch_<group>`)

After 30 days, double down on the 3 groups that converted best. Drop the 3 worst.

## Specific groups to research yourself

The list above is a starting point. **Search Facebook for these terms** and pick the most active groups (10k+ members, posts within last 24h):

- `metal vinyl collectors`
- `[your country] vinyl heavy metal`
- `[favorite band] fan group` (e.g. Gojira fans, Katatonia fans — many bands have 10-30k FB groups)
- `record collector [country/region]`
- `vinyl trading [genre]`
- Label-specific fan pages (Profound Lore, Season of Mist, Nuclear Blast — they sometimes have community FB pages)

**Tip**: Band fan groups convert better than generic vinyl groups. People who love a specific band are more likely to be obsessive enough to want a tracking app.
